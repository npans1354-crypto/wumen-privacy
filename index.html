WuMen — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

WuMen is a light, humorous app that “translates” everyday phrases between partners and gives friendly advice. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to the minimum needed to make the app work.

What we process

  • Text you type or speak for translation or advice is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our server and to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a response. It is used only to return that response. We do not store it, and it is not linked to your identity or used for advertising or tracking.
  • Speech recognition converts your voice to text. Where your device supports it, this happens on-device. Audio is not saved.
  • Photos (Mood scanner) are analyzed entirely on your device using Apple’s Vision framework. Photos are never uploaded and never stored by us. The Mood scanner is an entertainment feature and not a real emotion detector.

What we do NOT do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No advertising and no third-party analytics/tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

Third parties

Text for AI translation/advice is processed by Anthropic (Claude) solely to produce the response. Standard server logs may contain technical request information (such as IP address and timing) for security and reliability.

Children

WuMen is intended for a general audience (12+) and is not directed at children.
